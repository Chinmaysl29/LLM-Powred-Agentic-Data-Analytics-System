# Release Notes — AI Data Analyst OS

## Version 1.0.0 — Production General Availability (GA)
**Release Date**: September 2026  
**Status**: Production Ready & Certified

---

### Highlights & Platform Milestones
The **AI Data Analyst OS** represents a state-of-the-art, enterprise-grade AI operating system for full-lifecycle data analytics, predictive forecasting, natural language data querying, and strategic decision intelligence.

### Key Capabilities Delivered

#### 1. Core Analytics & Dataset Ingestion (Phases 1 - 3)
- Multi-format dataset ingestion: CSV, XLSX, Parquet, JSON, and SQL databases.
- Automated data profiling, quality scoring, missing value imputation, and anomaly detection.
- Statistical computation engine for descriptive metrics, hypothesis testing, and correlation matrices.

#### 2. SQL Intelligence & Guardrails (Phase 4)
- Text-to-SQL agent converting natural language business questions into ANSI SQL queries.
- Multi-layered SQL guardrails blocking 100% of destructive DDL (`DROP`, `ALTER`, `TRUNCATE`) and mutation (`DELETE`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`) commands.

#### 3. RAG Intelligence & Knowledge Retrieval (Phase 5)
- Hybrid RAG pipeline combining BM25 keyword search with dense embeddings.
- Table-aware, semantic, and recursive document chunking for corporate reports, financial statements, and business documentation.

#### 4. Time-Series Forecasting Intelligence (Phase 6)
- Multi-model ensemble with Auto-ARIMA, Facebook Prophet, and XGBoost.
- Automated backtesting, frequency detection, prediction intervals (95%), and scenario / what-if simulations.

#### 5. Decision & Recommendation Intelligence (Phase 7)
- Five specialized intelligence engines: Business Recommendations, Cost Optimization, Revenue Growth, Pricing Intelligence, and Inventory Optimization.
- Action prioritization engine ranking recommendations by ROI, implementation effort, and strategic impact.

#### 6. Enterprise Platform & Security (Phase 8)
- JWT token pair lifecycle with cryptographic refresh rotation and Redis blacklisting.
- Role-Based Access Control (Admin, Analyst, Viewer, Service).
- Enterprise observability with metrics collector, system health monitoring, and audit logging.

#### 7. Quality Engineering & Validation (Phase 9)
- 30-suite test framework across unit, integration, E2E, load testing (100 workers, 1000 reqs), and 17-point production pre-flight readiness audit.

#### 8. Deployment & Go-Live Automation (Phase 10)
- Zero-downtime deployment pipelines, automated rollbacks, SHA256 backup snapshots, Prometheus monitoring, and complete documentation suite.
